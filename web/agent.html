The agent's thoughts and actions appear here.
1 · Observe

The page in the frame is read through the DOM: numbered links, inputs and buttons, plus a window of its text.

2 · Decide

The local model gets the goal, the page and its previous steps (~1–2k tokens) and answers with one action.

3 · Act

The action runs on the DOM: click, type and submit, search the text, scroll, go back, or answer.

Measured: "When was the Eiffel Tower completed?" (2 steps, ~600 + ~1,400 prompt tokens)
